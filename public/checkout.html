CONFIRM RESERVATION

No payment needed here

Per the SRS, LocalStock doesn't process customer payments for products — a reservation is a free, 24-hour stock hold. This route is a placeholder for the "confirm your reservation" screen: item summary, pickup store, and a Confirm Reservation button that calls the reservation API instead of a payment gateway.

Retailer subscription billing is the one place a real payment gateway belongs (see REQ-SB-01) — that flow lives under /retailer/subscription, not here.
